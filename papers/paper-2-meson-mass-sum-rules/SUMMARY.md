# Meson mass sum rules in the six-flavour matrix

The $6\times6$ matrix organizes six tests. The pion and kaon GMOR relations, Gell-Mann–Okubo relation with neutral mixing, heavy-quark flavour splittings, additive $B_c$ estimate and heavy-light mass differences follow from established chiral, heavy-quark or additive approximations. The matrix makes the flavour assignments explicit.

The framework-specific scalar-democracy test is $m_s m_t/m_b^2\simeq1$. With all three running masses in the $\overline{\mathrm{MS}}$ scheme at $\mu=\bar m_t=162.9$ GeV, the ratio is **1.13**, a 13% departure. Using the strange mass at 2 GeV, bottom mass at its own scale and top pole mass gives 0.92. The latter mixes mass conventions. Unfixed coefficients limit predictions of individual masses.
