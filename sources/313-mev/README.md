## Spectroscopy

The tab-separated input lists masses followed by uncertainties in the order of the listed states. Each arithmetic mean is over electric charge states of one isospin multiplet. A single shared multiplet value enters only once. Charge-conjugate duplicates in the pion and rho triplets encode their multiplicities. The code allows for fully correlated inputs when giving conservative error bounds. The mixed-charge $\Delta$ entry of $1232$ MeV is the midpoint of the 1230–1234 Breit–Wigner range, and its 2 MeV half-range is descriptive. It is not used as a Gaussian error. The $K^*(892)^\pm$ entry is the hadroproduction value, the rho entries are Breit–Wigner, and the $B^*$ entry is the published multiplet mass. For the separate spin-one light-pair control, use the charged ends of each $\Sigma_c^{(*)}$ and $\Sigma_b^{(*)}$ isomultiplet consistently. [Input TSV](spectroscopy.tsv) · [2026 PDG meson tables](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2026/tables/rpp2026-sum-mesons.pdf) · [2026 PDG baryon tables](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2026/tables/rpp2026-sum-baryons.pdf)

Primary texts checked on 27 November 2026 include the mass definitions and statistical-only error convention of Francis et al., the earlier four-flavour relation of Karliner and Lipkin, the actual average used by Copeland, Ji and Melnitchouk, and the mass scan of Ali Khan et al. The 2026 family average is recalculated from the listed masses. The authors' 313 MeV octet–decuplet value uses their rounded inputs. Section 2 of the note gives the fit coordinate, number of fitted coefficients and the experimental-error bound for the bottom test. [Francis et al., table 1 and appendix A](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.09080) · [Karliner and Lipkin, §4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/0608004) · [Copeland et al., §II](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.03198) · [Ali Khan et al., table 17](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-lat/9912034)

For the other hadronic appearances, the verifier converts the published dimensionless fit $r_1\Lambda$ to MeV, inverts the leading pion evolution formula, and evaluates the two-pion invariant mass at fixed momenta. It does not repeat the underlying lattice simulations, global parton fits or dibaryon wavefunction calculation. The final uncertainties of those original analyses are quoted from their papers.
