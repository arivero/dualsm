Dualised Standard Model

A lecture course on Seiberg duality and the dualised Standard Model of Cacciapaglia and Sannino, in which colour is the magnetic gauge group; four research papers that extend it; the reference corpus; and a snapshot of related papers on the Koide relation. — A. Rivero, Universidad de Zaragoza.

📖 Lectures

11 lecture notes: anomaly matching, minimal SUSY, Seiberg duality, tumbling and the dualised Standard Model (1–8); the duality landscape and physical mesons (9–10); the Koide relation (11).

✏️ Exercises

9 problem sets with worked solutions; Set 9 accompanies Lecture 11.

📄 Papers

Four research papers: Sp(1) duality of the electroweak sector, meson mass sum rules, lattice signatures of emergent SUSY, and the number of generations.

🗂 Sources

23 arXiv reference papers used as background material.

🔗 Related Papers

Frozen 2026-03-22 snapshot: 37 TeX sources with PDFs from the Koide and sBootstrap notes (phys3–phys6) and this repository's papers.
