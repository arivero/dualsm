# Dualised Standard Model

The [papers](papers/index.md) and [lectures](lectures/index.md) test proposed relations among gauge duality, flavour masses and generation number. Extending supersymmetric duality to nonsupersymmetric QCD is conjectural.

| November 18 result | Basis and priority |
|---|---|
| Five-flavour electromagnetic matching | Holding the strong masses and weak VEV fixed leaves a free spectator $g_Y$. The inverse-cube dependence in the example is an elementary consequence. [Calculation](papers/paper-2-meson-mass-sum-rules/FIVE-FLAVOUR-MATCHING.md) |
| Direct and reciprocal Koide conditions | The unique positive spectrum up to scale and permutation is $(r^{-2},1,r^2)$, $r=(5+\sqrt{21})/2$. Elementary algebra. The archived alphaology chapter gives the same construction; wider priority is unknown. [Lecture 11](lectures/lecture-11.pdf) |
| Rank-two lift and common-mediator seesaw | The leading lift generically misses the Koide tangent; the three light–heavy pairs must lie on one line under the common-mass and alignment assumptions. Elementary tests in this form, with priority unestablished. [Derivations](papers/COMPOSITE-FLAVOUR-MATCHING.md) |
| Rigid Goldstino | Corrects the earlier sBootstrap example using the standard stationary-vacuum Ward identity. [Vacuum equations](papers/COMPOSITE-FLAVOUR-MATCHING.md#a-correction-to-the-parallel-oraifeartaigh-example) |
| Fundamental-only supercurrent exclusion | Combines baryon charge, unbroken charge conjugation and flavour-centre tests for local supercharges. The limited searches found no prior statement in this form. [Scope](papers/paper-3-emergent-susy-lattice/SUMMARY.md) |
| Same-rank seven- and eight-flavour SQCD endpoints | Direct application of the established $a$-theorem and SQCD anomaly. Equality of exponents alone is a separate benchmark. [Euler comparison](papers/paper-3-emergent-susy-lattice/paper.tex) |

The November results were author-checked on November 18. Two AI referees subsequently checked the listed derivations; strong-coupling dynamics, priority and extrapolation to the Standard Model remain open. No human peer review is recorded.
