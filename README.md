# Dualised Standard Model

The [papers](papers/index.md) and [lectures](lectures/index.md) test proposed relations among gauge duality, flavour masses and generation number. Extending supersymmetric duality to nonsupersymmetric QCD is conjectural.

| November 18 result | Basis and priority |
|---|---|
| Five-flavour electromagnetic matching | An elementary counterexample to a parameter-free mass ratio. At fixed strong masses, weak VEV and $g_w$, the independent spectator coupling gives $R\propto g_Y^{-3}$ for small $g_Y$. [Calculation](papers/paper-2-meson-mass-sum-rules/FIVE-FLAVOUR-MATCHING.md) |
| Direct and reciprocal Koide conditions | For the same positive triple the unique spectrum up to scale and permutation is $(r^{-2},1,r^2)$, $r=(5+\sqrt{21})/2$. The archived chapter contains this elementary construction. Rivero's published inverse rule concerns the down-quark triple. Wider priority for the simultaneous result is open. [Archive](sources/alphaology-shape.md) · [Rivero, PLB](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10060) |
| Rank-two lift and common-mediator seesaw | Turning on a second amplitude with the first fixed gives zero slope for the heavy ratio at onset. The Koide curve requires $2+2\sqrt3$. A separate determinant and trace test puts the three light–heavy pairs on one line for aligned mixings and a common mediator mass. Both are elementary consequences of their hypotheses. Priority for these tests is open. [Derivations](papers/COMPOSITE-FLAVOUR-MATCHING.md) · [Universal seesaw background](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0209148) |
| Finite-mixing Koide shift and mediator reconstruction | In the aligned common-mediator block, every finite mediator mass raises the Koide ratio above an unequal seed, to all orders in the mixing. At fixed mixing asymmetry a seed value is realized by exactly one mediator mass if and only if it lies strictly between $\mathcal Q(m^2)$ and $\mathcal Q(m)$, a window fixed by the light masses alone. The block algebra is textbook universal seesaw, and the leading shift is already in the alphaology source. The all-orders sign and the inverse at fixed $\kappa$ are elementary and new as stated, with priority open. For charged leptons with an exact $3/2$ seed the pole masses give only a lower bound on the mediator mass, and at a common scale the seed overshoots. AI reviewers checked the derivation by hand and by script in issue 3; independent review is pending. [Derivation](papers/COMPOSITE-FLAVOUR-MATCHING.md#finite-mixing-the-exact-koide-shift-and-the-reconstructed-mediator-mass) · [Check script](scripts/verify_finite_mixing_koide.py) · [Issue 3](https://github.com/arivero/dualsm/issues/3) |
| Rigid Goldstino | A correction to the project's earlier example using the standard stationary-vacuum Ward identity. The old seed ceases to be stationary, and the deformation admits two supersymmetric vacua. [Vacuum equations](papers/COMPOSITE-FLAVOUR-MATCHING.md#a-correction-to-the-parallel-oraifeartaigh-example) · [Corrected manuscript](https://github.com/arivero/physres2/blob/6027cdc/sbootstrap_v4d.tex) |
| Fundamental-only supercurrent exclusion | The faithful QCD symmetry and fermion counting are established ingredients. Combining them with unbroken unitary charge conjugation to exclude one local Weyl supercharge is a candidate contribution. The searches recorded in the review establish limited coverage and leave priority open. [Proposition and hypotheses](papers/paper-3-emergent-susy-lattice/SUMMARY.md#which-theories-can-support-a-supercurrent) · [Tanizaki, §2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.07666) |
| Same-rank seven- and eight-flavour SQCD endpoints | Direct application of the four-dimensional $a$-theorem and the SQCD Euler anomaly. At eight flavours $\gamma_m=1/8$ remains an exponent benchmark with no known matching mechanism in fundamental-only QCD. [Endpoint calculation](papers/paper-3-emergent-susy-lattice/paper.tex) · [SQCD anomalies](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9708042) · [$a$-theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.3987) |

The authors checked these results on 18 November. Two internal AI referees reported their hand checks on 19–20 November. Independent peer review is pending. The review record gives the checks claim by claim, including the strong-coupling assumptions and the precision of the numerical comparisons.
