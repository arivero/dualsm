## Why Keep/Revise/Replace
The first-pass assessment identifies the right lecture center. This is the quantitative core of the dualised SM narrative, and the assessment correctly tracks the move from universal Yukawa structure to Planck-suppressed flavour operators, multi-Higgs electroweak breaking, scale matching, and collider signatures. Its praise for the scalar-democracy explanation and for the order-of-magnitude scale checks is well supported by the source.

The revision need is mostly about emphasis. The first pass notices that the lecture distinguishes consistency checks from predictions, but it treats that restraint as a side virtue when the source makes it a dominant organizing principle. The convention box frames the entire lecture in terms of parametric consistency with `O(1)` coefficients. Multiple warning boxes repeat "not prediction" language for the top mass, the Fermi scale relation, and the QCD-scale estimate. The late "What the dualised SM does not do" section states in one place that the framework does not prove the duality, does not predict specific fermion masses, does not predict `\Lambda_D`, and does not resolve Cheng-Shadmi. Because of that, the first pass overstates rhetorical overreach.

Its notation concern is only partly fair. The `246 GeV` versus `174 GeV` burden is real for readers, but the source explicitly reconciles them by defining `v^2 = (246 GeV)^2 / 2` and then remarking that `v = 246/\sqrt{2} GeV`. The more source-grounded weakness is the density of the operator-to-mass-mixing-to-induced-VEV chain, where a reader can genuinely lose the causal thread.

## Minimum Changes Needed
- Promote the lecture's anti-overclaim discipline to a primary strength.
- Shift the main criticism away from rhetoric and toward the density of the Planck-operator to dormant-Higgs to induced-VEV sequence.
- Soften the notation complaint from "internally confusing" to "readability-heavy but explained."
- Note explicitly that the free coefficients `c^{abcd}`, `\xi`, and `\Lambda_D` are already foregrounded as inputs.

## Notes For Final Summary
Useful assessment, but it underestimates how aggressively the source already polices the difference between naturalness, consistency, and prediction.
