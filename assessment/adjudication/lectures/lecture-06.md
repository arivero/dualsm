## Why Keep/Revise/Replace
The first-pass assessment captures the lecture's core intellectual role very well. Lecture 6 is indeed the epistemic hinge of the course, and the assessment is right that its strongest feature is the refusal to hide the central weakness of non-SUSY duality. The source repeatedly states that anomaly matching is exact while the duality interpretation is conjectural, and the historical sequence identified in the first pass matches the document's actual progression.

Still, the first pass needs revision because it understates how formalized that epistemic discipline is in the source. This is not just a matter of careful tone. The convention box explicitly installs conjectural qualification as a lecture-wide rule, and the file header labels that rule "NON-NEGOTIABLE." The assessment also criticizes the phrase "only anomaly matching survives" as too absolute, but in context the source is clearly reserving "survives" for exact non-perturbative pillars. Perturbative fixed points, Veneziano-limit evidence, and large-`N` arguments are presented later as suggestive support, not as surviving pillars on the same footing.

A second correction is that one of the first-pass suggestions is already implemented: the preview box for Lectures 7-8 explicitly says later lectures will assume the conjecture as a working hypothesis and that every later prediction is conditional. Likewise, the Cheng-Shadmi discussion is more concrete than the first pass suggests. The lecture does not merely raise a vague incompatibility; it identifies tachyonic magnetic scalars and the resulting wrong symmetry-breaking pattern in the large SUSY-breaking regime.

## Minimum Changes Needed
- Elevate the lecture's epistemic contract from a stylistic virtue to an explicit structural convention.
- Narrow the criticism of "only anomaly matching survives" so it reflects the lecture's exact-versus-suggestive distinction.
- Replace the generic Cheng-Shadmi concern with the specific unresolved issue the source names.
- Remove the suggestion that later lectures should declare their conditional status, because the preview already does.

Strong first pass overall, but it misses that the source has already formalized several of the cautions it recommends.
