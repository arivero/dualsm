## Why Keep/Revise/Replace
The first-pass assessment contains a couple of genuine observations, but overall it is materially unreliable because large parts of it do not match the file that actually exists. Its strongest accurate point is the `R`-symmetry criticism in Problem 1: the source says the `\mathcal N=2` and `\mathcal N=1` `R`-symmetries "coincide" at `N_f=2N_c`, then immediately evaluates the `\mathcal N=1` value as `1/2`, not `1` (`267-274`). The note that the self-duality discussion is compressed is also defensible, since Problem 3 moves quickly from integrating out `\Phi` to inherited self-duality (`687-829`).

Those correct points are not enough to save the assessment. The structural mismatch is too large. The source has three problems only: Problem 1 on `\mathcal N=2` decomposition, Problem 2 on the Fayet-Iliopoulos `D`-term, and Problem 3 on the APS deformation and `SU(2), N_f=4` self-dual case (`59`, `285`, `531`). The first pass repeatedly refers to a "Problem 4" and a "final section on the conformal window" as if they were separate units. They are not. The conformal-window material appears only in Problem 3(e), and it concerns the exact `\mathcal N=1` SQCD window for `SU(2)` with `N_f=4` (`595-606`, `782-800`), not unsettled non-SUSY evidence.

It also overstates ambiguity in Problem 2. The FI-term exercise is internally consistent and quite explicit about vacuum structure, dimensional analysis, and Higgs-mass extraction (`307-523`). If one wants to flag heuristic content, the right target is the monopole-condensation analogy in Problem 2(e), not a nonexistent later problem.

Because the assessment misstates the number of problems and imports a non-SUSY conformal-window critique into a supersymmetric sheet, it reads as though it was written against a different draft.
