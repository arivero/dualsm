## Why Keep/Revise/Replace
The first-pass assessment captures the paper's main strengths and its most serious vulnerabilities, so it should be revised rather than replaced. It is right that the route-by-route structure is clear, that the SU and Sp derivations are the mechanically strongest parts, and that the paper's headline conclusion is stronger than the evidence strictly warrants. It is also right to focus on the Pati-Salam boundary case: the source explicitly computes `b_0 = 0` for `n_g = 3`, notes that this sits at the upper boundary of asymptotic freedom, and still treats that case as viable. That is a real fragility and a legitimate target for criticism.

The assessment nevertheless needs correction in three places. First, it understates how much the paper already qualifies itself. The abstract says all non-SUSY-duality claims are conjectural, the introduction contains an explicit "Epistemic convention" paragraph, the "Robustness of the `n_g = 3` selection" section says the Sp upper bound depends on importing the SUSY conformal window, and the "Model dependence" section lists flavor-counting and embedding choices openly. So the right charge is not that the paper leaves its assumptions implicit; it is that the title, abstract, and conclusion use theorem-like language that is stronger than the already-qualified body.

Second, the first pass somewhat overstates the role of SO and Kutasov-Schwimmer in the actual proof structure. In the source, the decisive step is the SU lower bound intersected with the Sp upper bound. SO is presented as an excluded route, and KS with `k >= 2` is surveyed mainly as a dead end. Those sections may still be overextended, but they are not load-bearing in the final `n_g = 3` selection.

Third, the boundary-case criticism should be sharpened, not broadened. The paper is explicit that it includes boundary cases in the master-table caption and explicitly ties the Pati-Salam `n_g = 3` inclusion to a cited literature convention. The weakness is permissiveness, not concealment.

## Minimum Changes Needed
1. Recast the main objection as a mismatch between strong headline rhetoric and a more qualified body, rather than as a generic failure to state assumptions.
2. Keep the `b_0 = 0` Pati-Salam criticism, but note that boundary inclusion is explicit and literature-backed rather than silently smuggled in.
3. Re-center the critique on the actual proof spine: SU lower bound plus Sp upper bound.
4. Treat SO and higher-`k` KS as secondary survey material whose exclusions are informative but not decisive.

## Notes For Final Summary
Solid first draft that identifies the real pressure points, especially the permissive boundary handling and overstrong uniqueness rhetoric, but it needs to reflect that the paper's own body is more self-qualified and more focused on SU plus Sp than the first pass suggests.
