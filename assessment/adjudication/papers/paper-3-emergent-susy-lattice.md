## Confidence
High

## Why Keep/Revise/Replace
The first-pass assessment is directionally sound and should not be discarded. It correctly identifies the paper's strongest material: the effort to turn the emergent-SUSY conjecture into lattice-facing observables, especially the `gamma_m` program in Section 3 and Observable 3, plus the scalar-to-pseudoscalar ratio discussion in Observable 1. It also correctly isolates Observables 2, 4, and 5 as the weakest parts of the manuscript. That criticism is source-grounded: the paper itself says Observable 2 is "not directly available" in the target fundamental-fermion theory, the Ward-identity proposal depends on constructing a composite supercurrent in the electric theory, and the flat-direction discussion imports SUSY moduli-space logic into a non-SUSY setting.

It still needs revision because it overstates one important charge and misplaces one point of praise. The assessment implies the paper regularly slides past its own conjectural status, but the source repeatedly marks the claims as conditional: the abstract says all emergent-SUSY predictions are conjectural, the introduction says the measurements are meant to test rather than assume the conjecture, Section 3 opens by stating all results there are conditional, and the prediction-table caption again says all SUSY values are conjectural. The better criticism is not "the paper forgets uncertainty," but "the paper sometimes pushes speculative observables too hard despite openly stating the hypothesis is conditional."

The first pass also treats "volume dependence" as part of the comparatively coherent core. That is not quite right. The explicit volume-scaling proposal belongs mainly to Observable 4, the SUSY Ward-identity section, which is one of the least well-founded parts. On discriminating power, the first pass has a real point, but it should acknowledge that the source already says emergent SUSY versus ordinary conformal dynamics requires at least two observables at the same `Nf`, so the paper does not fully present the five probes as five independent proofs.

## Minimum Changes Needed
1. Reframe the main criticism as overextension from a conditional emergent-SUSY ansatz into weakly justified observables, not as a general failure to flag conjectural status.
2. Remove or qualify the praise of "volume dependence" as a strong feature; in the source it is tied mainly to the speculative Ward-identity observable.
3. Keep the criticism of rhetorical overreach around `Nf = 8`, but note that the source already preserves the dilaton alternative and does not claim decisive evidence.
4. Distinguish the paper's two strongest proposal pillars, `gamma_m` and `m_sigma / m_pi`, from the three auxiliary speculative extensions.

## Notes For Final Summary
Useful first pass with good instincts on where the paper is robust versus speculative, but it needs tighter alignment to the manuscript's own caveat structure and to the hierarchy among the five observables.
