## Checks on the derivations listed by the referees

- **Composite flavour.** The positive Koide branch has $b(0)=2+\sqrt3$ and $b'(0)=2+2\sqrt3$. Holding the first amplitude fixed gives $m_e/m_\star=\epsilon^2\lVert Bn\rVert^2+O(\epsilon^4)$ and zero slope for the heavy ratio. The displayed diagonal deformation supplies the required slope by varying both amplitudes. The Fourier triplet gives the quoted trace ratio, light amplitude and heavy endpoints. The projection difference is a squared norm, and the degree-one bundle $L(-p-q)$ has one holomorphic section. For each seesaw block the determinant and squared norm give the common line of slope at least two. For the positive $2\times3$ array the two independent unit double ratios are equivalent to factorization. Substitution in the three F-equations gives the two supersymmetric vacua. The gradient at the old seed is nonzero for $\epsilon f\ne0$. [Derivations and counterexample](../../papers/COMPOSITE-FLAVOUR-MATCHING.md)
- **Five-flavour matching.** The electric hypercharges are $2/3,-1/3$ and the magnetic fundamental charges are $-1/2,+1/2$. Inverting the two-by-two transverse kernel recovers the massless residue, both vector masses and $\Pi_Y=f^2/(t+g_m^2f^2)$. The surviving magnetic components independently give the electric infrared count $8+2+2=12$. On the full-rank branch $S^3=\Lambda^4\det m$ gives the holomorphic charged Hessian $m_im_j/S$. The spectator ratio scales as $g_Y^{-3}$ at small $g_Y$. The meson charges have multiplicities $(12,6,6)$, twice the left-Weyl count for three chiral lepton generations with $\nu_R$. [Branch calculations and counterexample](../../papers/paper-2-meson-mass-sum-rules/FIVE-FLAVOUR-MATCHING.md)
- **Lecture 11.** On the ordered arc $0\le\delta\le\pi/12$, $2/9$ lies in the interior. Fixing the first two elementary symmetric polynomials leaves the degree-five potential linear in $e_3=z_0^3(\sqrt2\cos3\delta-1)/2$. Simultaneous direct and reciprocal Koide gives $(t-1)(t^2-5t+1)$. Two last-digit differences in the report can be resolved from the inputs. At $\delta=2/9$ the adjacent mass ratios are $0.0048362841$ and $0.0594599370$. For down-quark masses $(4.70,93.5,4183)$ MeV, the reciprocal $Q$ is $1.50454929$, which rounds to the lecture's $1.5045$. Rivero's PLB article studies the empirical inverse sum for down quarks. The positive simultaneous solution is explicit in the archived chapter. [Lecture and inputs](../../lectures/lecture-11.tex) · [Archived derivation](../../sources/alphaology-shape.md) · [PLB article](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.10060)
- **Paper 3.** At $(3,8)$ the mass sources the meson's top component. The charges and dimensions give $R(Q)=5/8$, $\Delta_{F_M}=23/8$, $y_m=9/8$ and $\gamma_m=1/8$. The four scaling powers in the report follow from $4/y_m$, $4/y_m-1$, $2\Delta_{F_M}$ and $1/y_m$. The colour and flavour centres give the parity and divisibility tests. Conjugation requires $b=-b$ for a unique Weyl charge. The sum and difference of the two mixed anomalies give $b=0,u=1$ for the standard SQCD embedding. Substitution at five through eight flavours reproduces all eight Euler differences, including $17/2880,41/320$ at six, and the three eight-flavour anomalies $19/9,2535/1024,5/2$ for the specified theories. In the adjoint-plus-six example $b_0=5$, $b_1=-22$ and the formal zero is $10\pi/11$. The axial colour anomaly vanishes. The $X$ charges of $O,P,\lambda\psi$ are $-1,+1,+1/2$. The conditional two-point powers for $P,O$ are $3,5$. Ryttov–Shrock Table VI gives the two-loop fundamental-QCD values $4.19$ at ten flavours and $0.773$ at twelve. At eight there is no positive two-loop zero. [Paper 3](../../papers/paper-3-emergent-susy-lattice/paper.tex) · [Tanizaki, §2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.07666) · [SQCD Euler anomaly, Eq. (4.23)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9708042) · [Ryttov–Shrock](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1011.4542)
