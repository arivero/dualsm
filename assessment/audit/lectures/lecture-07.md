# Audit: Lecture 07 Assessment

## Source Pair
`lectures/lecture-07.tex` and `assessment/lectures/lecture-07.md`

## Verdict
Accurate but incomplete.

## What The Assessment Gets Right
The assessment is right about the lecture's main scaffold. The source does proceed from a general SU(N) electric/magnetic duality ansatz, to the Pati-Salam embedding and hypercharge formula, to the specialized N = 3, N_f = 6 spectrum, and then to an extended anomaly-matching check. Its praise of the hypercharge table is justified: the source explicitly checks all six Standard Model hypercharges and ties the relation Y = T_R^3 + Q_V/6 to the usual Y = T_R^3 + (B-L)/2 form. The assessment is also right that the generation bound n_g >= 3 is framework-dependent rather than a Standard Model theorem. The lecture itself repeatedly qualifies that conclusion with "if the duality holds."

It is also fair to flag the transition from representation matching to stronger compositeness claims. The text does identify M and Phi_H as electric composites and then reads the Higgs sector through that interpretation. That is more than bare bookkeeping, even though the lecture keeps repeating that the dual interpretation is conjectural.

## What It Misses Or Overstates
The biggest overstatement is the claim that the QCD-sector duality and the Pati-Salam extension are not kept sharp enough. The source directly anticipates that issue. It includes a warning box explicitly distinguishing the QCD-sector duality, where leptons are elementary states added for anomaly cancellation, from the full Pati-Salam extension, where leptons are the fourth color. It even states which framework the lecture is following. So the conceptual fork is real, but it is not left unmarked.

What the assessment misses is how much of the lecture is devoted to anomaly bookkeeping. Nearly the entire final section computes six anomaly conditions for SU(3)_c x SU(2)_L x U(1)_Y and then reiterates that anomaly matching is necessary but not sufficient. That is not a side check; it is one of the lecture's main structural pillars.

## Recommended Revisions
Revise the assessment to acknowledge that the source already contains an explicit "two statuses" distinction between the QCD-sector presentation and the Pati-Salam completion. Replace the claim that this fork is insufficiently signposted with a narrower point: the lecture still moves quickly between SU(3)_c, SU(4)_PS, and the electric SU(2) dual, so readers may need more help tracking which gauge group is active in a given subsection. Add a note that the lecture is not just model-building and tables; it is also a long anomaly-cancellation exercise, and that feature should be part of any accurate audit.
