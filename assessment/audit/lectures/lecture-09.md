# Audit: Lecture 09 Assessment

## Source Pair
`lectures/lecture-09.tex` and `assessment/lectures/lecture-09.md`

## Verdict
Mostly accurate, but some concerns are overstated.

## What The Assessment Gets Right
The assessment is correct about the lecture's breadth. The source covers Kutasov-Schwimmer duality, SO duality, Sp duality, s-confinement, product-group duality, and the Klebanov-Strassler cascade, then closes with a synthesis table. It is also right that the explicit anomaly-matching calculations for SO and Sp are an important strength. The lecture does not simply quote the dual gauge groups; it works through representative SU(N_f)^2 U(1)_R and SU(2N_f)^2 U(1)_R checks in detail and even includes numerical spot checks. The assessment is also justified in praising the final synthesis section. The master comparison table and the closing relevance discussion make the lecture more useful than a disconnected list of famous results.

## What It Misses Or Overstates
The main overstatement is the claim that the lecture "sometimes reads like a well-curated list" rather than a single argument. The source has a stronger through-line than that. From the recap onward, it frames the question as whether Seiberg duality is special to SU(N) fundamentals, then answers that by moving through systematic generalizations. It states in advance that Kutasov-Schwimmer is the most relevant generalization for the DSM, uses one explicit anomaly check per new gauge family, and returns to the DSM again in both the preview and synthesis sections.

The source also already implements some of the assessment's suggestions. It effectively does include "why this family matters" signals: the opening preview box identifies KS as the direct bridge to the DSM, and the synthesis section explains why SO and Sp matter as evidence of structural breadth rather than direct model input. Likewise, the final relevance paragraph already distinguishes which ingredients matter most for the DSM and for Lecture 10.

What the assessment underplays is the amount of convention work the source must do to make the survey coherent, especially the Sp(N) naming warning and the changed Dynkin-index normalizations for SO versus SU/Sp.

## Recommended Revisions
Revise the assessment from "broad survey that risks becoming a list" to "broad survey with a clear anomaly-matching and generalization through-line." Keep the point that the product-group and cascade sections move quickly, because that is true, but note that the lecture labels them as extensions rather than as equally central ingredients. Add that the source already singles out Kutasov-Schwimmer as the directly relevant family for the dualised Standard Model, while using SO and Sp primarily to demonstrate the generality of the duality phenomenon.
