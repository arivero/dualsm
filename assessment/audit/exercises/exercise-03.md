# Exercise 03 Audit

## Source Pair
`exercises/exercise-03.tex` and `assessment/exercises/exercise-03.md`

## Verdict
Mostly accurate, but incomplete.

## What The Assessment Gets Right
The assessment matches the sheet's real ambition and pacing. The source is technically denser than Exercises 1 and 2, and it does ask students to move through explicit anomaly matching, mass deformation, `R`-charge derivations, and `s`-confinement in one sheet. The praise for Problem 1 is justified: the TeX front-loads full quantum-number tables for electric and magnetic theories, including superfield and fermion `R`-charges, before asking for anomaly arithmetic. That is a sound pedagogical choice. The comments on Problem 2 are also grounded in the source. The solution derives the `F`-term condition `q_6 \tilde q^6 = -\mu m` and states that a nonzero `q_6` VEV Higgses `SU(3)` to `SU(2)`, but it does not explain the gauge orientation, unbroken subgroup, or which modes are eaten. The critique that Problem 3's `R`-charge derivation is somewhat under-scaffolded is likewise fair: the logic is compact and expects students to fill in some group-theory and anomaly-cancellation steps.

## What It Misses Or Overstates
The main omission is Problem 4. The assessment never mentions that the worked dimensional analysis there contains an explicit wrong start followed by a correction. The source first states in the hint that mesons have dimension `[mass]`, repeats that assumption in the solution, obtains the wrong answer `[mass]^2` for the superpotential, then stops and corrects itself by reassigning the meson dimension to `[mass]^2`. That is a more concrete and potentially confusing issue than the assessment's general concern about under-explained conventions. There is also a smaller technical slip in the Problem 3 background, where the conformal-dimension formula is written with an absolute value around `R`; the solution then uses the standard expression without the absolute value. The assessment's current concerns are sound, but they do not capture these source-level corrections and inconsistencies.

## Recommended Revisions
Keep the current comments on Problems 1 to 3, but add a specific note that Problem 4 includes a self-correction in the dimensional analysis of the confining superpotential. That deserves explicit mention because it affects the reliability of a worked solution, not just the pacing. The revised assessment should recommend either fixing the hint and initial dimension assignment for `M`, or reframing the wrong start as an intentional diagnostic checkpoint. It would also help to note that the sheet is not merely dense; it contains at least one worked calculation that visibly changes its own assumptions midstream. That is the main gap in the current first-pass assessment.
