# Audit of Exercise 8 Assessment

## Source Pair
`exercises/exercise-08.tex` and `assessment/exercises/exercise-08.md`

## Verdict
Accurate overall, but incomplete. The assessment captures the main pedagogical strengths and the core caution about over-claiming duality interpretations, though it misses a few concrete slips in the worked solutions.

## What The Assessment Gets Right
The assessment matches the file's structure well. Problem 1 is a quantum-number and meson-identification exercise, Problem 2 moves to scalar versus pseudoscalar modes and the GMOR relation, and Problem 3 reconnects the meson discussion to the Higgs-VEV hierarchy. That progression is genuinely present in the source and is one of its strengths.

Its central substantive criticism is also well grounded. The source repeatedly says that the duality interpretation is conjectural, but Problem 2(a)(i) still identifies the scalar fluctuation `sigma_12` directly with `a_0(980)^+`, and Problem 2(c) offers a fairly strong dual-language formulation for the pion/dual-meson relation. The assessment is right that those statements need more caution than the standard QCD material surrounding them.

The note on Problem 3 is also fair. Part 3(b) asks whether one geometric suppression parameter `epsilon` can reproduce four different quark-mass ratios, and the solution answers by absorbing the spread into `O(1)` coefficients. That is a reasonable parametric argument, but the prompt does not foreground that freedom strongly enough.

## What It Misses Or Overstates
The assessment understates how wobbly the charm-meson discussion becomes in Problem 1(d). The solution first tries to apply the generalized Gell-Mann-Nishijima formula to `D^0` and momentarily gets `Q = 1/2`, then corrects itself by changing the isospin assignment. That is more than an unnecessary aside; it is evidence that the charge/quantum-number explanation is less controlled in this subsection than elsewhere.

It also does not mention a smaller but real source of confusion in Problem 1(a): the matrix labels the `(3,3)` entry as `eta_s`, even though part 1(c) later explains that the physical neutral states are mixtures yielding `pi^0`, `eta`, and `eta'`, not simply unmixed diagonal quark states.

## Recommended Revisions
Keep the existing emphasis on over-claiming, but add two concrete examples from Problem 1: the self-correcting `D^0` charge discussion and the potentially confusing `eta_s` label in the initial matrix. That would sharpen the audit by tying its cautionary judgment to explicit points where the source text itself becomes uneven.
