# Lecture 07 Assessment

## Source
`lectures/lecture-07.tex`

## Scope
This lecture constructs the dualised Standard Model at the level of field content, symmetry assignments, and anomaly matching. It is the first lecture where the conjectured non-SUSY duality is used as a working framework rather than just a possibility.

## Strengths
The lecture is conceptually ambitious but mostly well scaffolded. The progression from the general duality ansatz to Pati--Salam embedding, then to the magnetic spectrum and electric theory, gives the reader a usable map of the model. The hypercharge check is especially effective because it turns an abstract identification into a concrete table of Standard Model values.

The spectrum tables are also pedagogically useful. They show, in one place, which fields are intended as SM degrees of freedom and which are quasi-SUSY partners. The lecture does a good job of reminding the reader that the field identifications are group-theoretic and conjectural, not dynamically proven.

## Concerns
- The lecture alternates between a QCD-sector duality and a Pati--Salam extension, and the distinction is not always kept sharp enough. In places, leptons are elementary bookkeeping states; in others, they are treated as fourth-colour components. That is a real conceptual fork and should be signposted more aggressively.
- The generation bound $n_g \ge 3$ is presented as a structural requirement, but it depends on the specific duality construction rather than on the Standard Model in general. The lecture should make the model-dependence more explicit.
- The jump from field tables to claims like “all Higgs doublets are composite” is understandable in the framework, but it is still a major inference. The lecture could more clearly separate representation matching from the dynamical claim of compositeness.

## Suggestions
- Add an early “two embeddings, two statuses” box that contrasts the QCD-sector duality with the Pati--Salam version and states what each is used for in this lecture.
- Introduce a one-page symbol map for the electric and magnetic spectra. There are many repeated labels, and a compact glossary would help preserve the argument’s flow.
- Rephrase the most ambitious claims as conditional conclusions, especially when moving from anomaly matching to the interpretation of the Higgs sector.
